# Release Room

Operate mode. A publisher's proof desk for developers checking a synthetic release while sorting customer feedback. The workbench has two independent ledgers, not a single combined readiness score.

Warm ivory (#f5f0e6) is the working paper, deep aubergine (#322133) carries text and rules, and vermilion (#b63825) marks decisions and selected evidence. Newsreader is self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License in static/OFL.txt. Functional controls use the platform sans serif. Continuous rules, asymmetric columns, and a right proof margin establish the hierarchy. No gradients, decorative icons, or uniform card grid.

Desktop shows feedback on the left and release evidence on the right. Below 1050px the desks stack. Below 560px the proof margin joins the reading flow. Labels, native controls, visible focus, live status messages, and plain-language error recovery support keyboard and mobile use. Judgment freshness belongs to each desk independently. Product, evidence, mode, and text changes clear their affected judgment and ignore older in-flight responses.
